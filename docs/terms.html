My rclone Terms of Use

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

My rclone is an owner-operated personal backup setup using rclone and Google Drive. It is not a public storage service and has no subscription fee or public sign-up.

Only transfer files you own or are authorized to transfer. You remain responsible for your account permissions, available storage and checking backup completeness. Google Drive and Apple iCloud are subject to their respective providers' terms.

The setup is provided as-is without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation or complete backups. Keep independent copies of important files. You may stop using it by stopping the jobs and revoking Google account access; existing backups remain until you delete them.

This private configuration is maintained by its account owner. See the privacy policy.
